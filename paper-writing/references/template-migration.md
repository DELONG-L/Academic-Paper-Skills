# Prepare or Migrate a Manuscript Template

Use for a supplied template archive, a new paper template, venue conversion, or
submission packaging. Verify the target year, track, article type and stage from
the supplied current template or official instructions. Repository template
examples and remembered page limits are not current venue authority.

## Establish a Working Baseline

Keep the original archive or manuscript. Inspect archive members before extraction
and reject paths escaping the destination. Extract into a separate working area.
Identify the entry document, class/style files, bibliography backend, engine,
macros, figures, and include tree. Compile the supplied example before migrating
content so template problems can be distinguished from introduced problems.

## Transfer Content and Dependencies

Start from the target template. Transfer section content, bibliography and assets;
review necessary custom macros/packages individually instead of combining two
preambles wholesale. Preserve official class and style files. Do not alter margins,
font sizes, spacing or template internals to simulate compliance.

Preserve equations, labels, citation keys and material content while adapting the
organization. Record file moves and update their consumers. Do not remove files
merely because they look like examples until their dependencies and purpose are
known. A clean target copy is usually easier to review than destructive cleanup.

For page or word pressure, use `paper-review`'s argument-structure guidance to
remove redundancy and move suitable detail. Keep evidence needed to assess the
main claims. More available space does not require invented experiments or filler.

## Check the Actual Submission Requirements

Use `venue-adaptation.md` for the current requirement record. Check anonymity,
page/word counting rules, required statements, bibliography, supplement and
figure specifications against official instructions. Apply self-citation rules
from the target venue; do not automatically replace real authors with Anonymous.

For journal delivery, prepare requested cover letter, availability statements,
reporting forms, response document, and clean/marked manuscripts as applicable.
Author declarations, approvals, conflicts, suggested reviewers and repository
availability must come from verified facts or author confirmation. Do not invent
them to complete a form.

## Verify and Deliver

Compile the migrated manuscript and inspect its rendered pages at final size.
Check references, bibliography, math, figures/tables, page count and anonymity.
For a source package, extract a copy into a fresh directory and rebuild; include
necessary dependencies and exclude irrelevant private records or credentials.

Deliver the source/package, rendered document and concise build/requirement notes.
Only claim formal readiness after the requested `paper-policy` assessment. Local
preparation does not authorize submission or remote publication.
